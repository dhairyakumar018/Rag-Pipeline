# Company Remote Work & Benefits Policy

## Remote Work Guidelines
Employees are eligible for remote work after completing their 90-day onboarding period.
Full-time remote employees receive a **$500 home office setup stipend** upon joining.
Core collaboration hours are between **10:00 AM and 4:00 PM EST**.

## Health & Wellness Benefits
- Premium health, dental, and vision insurance covered at 90% by the company.
- $100 monthly wellness allowance for gym memberships, sports, or mental health apps.
- Unlimited Paid Time Off (PTO) with a mandatory minimum of 15 days per year.
