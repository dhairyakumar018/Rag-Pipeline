## What I Want in an Internship

**Type of work**
- Real engineering work — building and shipping, not certificate-collecting
- Full-stack: React / Next.js / TypeScript on the front, Python / Node / FastAPI on the back
- Backend or full-stack preferred; open to AI/ML-adjacent work
- Small, early-stage teams where I actually own things

**Terms**
- **Paid** — realistic band ₹8–20K/month for a fresher
- Remote or hybrid (hostel-based, 9–5 college Mon–Fri, alternate Saturdays off)
- Happy to start with a **small paid trial task** so the employer takes zero risk

**Deal-breakers**
- Unpaid "exposure" roles
- Pure gig work with no skill growth (data annotation, AI evaluation, form filling) — pays but builds nothing
- Anything that eats the deep-work blocks reserved for open source / GSoC prep

**How I find them**
- Cold DMs to founders (LinkedIn post search sorted by Latest, Product Hunt launches, YC directory, #buildinpublic on X)
- Wellfound / Internshala / Cuvette — paid filter only
- NiET alumni founders (highest reply rate)
- Every DM must reference something specific and true about *their* product — no detail, no send
- Target: 5–10 quality DMs/week, one follow-up after 7 days, then let it go

## What I Want in Freelancing

**Yes**
- Client websites I actually build — ₹5–15K per simple site, ~1 weekend each
- Local first: coaching centres, gyms, cafés, family businesses, campus network
- Work that compounds — each build makes the next easier and the portfolio stronger
- Platforms worth trying: Wellfound, Contra (dev-focused, not gig mills)

**No**
- Hours-for-rupees gig work with no skill growth
- Racing to the bottom on Fiverr/Upwork as an unknown — only worth it with a hyper-specific niche ("fast landing pages on Next.js + Cloudflare")

**Capacity rule**
- One client at a time until I know my real bandwidth
- Fits in the Sunday project slot + at most one weekday evening
- Client work has deadlines and calls — it is *less* flexible than it looks

## The Standing Principles

1. **What I've built beats CGPA** — for startups, shipped work is the resume
2. **Cold outreach beats job boards** — specificity is the whole trick
3. **Concentration beats scatter** — one org, one platform, one client at a time
4. **Skill-building work > hours-for-rupees work** — always, at this stage
5. **Never miss twice** — one skipped day is fine, two is how streaks die
6. **The schedule serves the goals, not the reverse**
